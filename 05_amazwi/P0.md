## THE THEME — you do not have to choose today

Lethabo asked to keep the ability to switch rather than commit. **A pre-event reference implementation exists:** `04_assets/themes/tokens.css` defines all five as `[data-theme]` blocks. The intended on-site implementation is one line:

```js
document.documentElement.dataset.theme = 'dusk';   // shweshwe | dusk | earth | ndebele | ink
```

**So the decision moves from "pick one now" to "choose after the target-device contrast check."** The pre-event theme demo proves a reference approach only. Wire the actual competition app against tokens on-site, never hex, and keep the choice open until the real target-device check.

**Three constraints that hold whatever gets picked:**
1. **`earth` ships regardless.** It is the Day theme and it is an accessibility requirement, not an aesthetic option.
2. **`dusk` is deck and splash only** — small text over a mesh gradient is a contrast fight you lose.
3. **`ndebele` is seasonal**, and if it is used the attribution is said out loud.

**For Sbu and Lethabo on-site:** review the grounds on the actual target device and pick three you would ship. Lethabo makes the final experience call from that shortlist.
