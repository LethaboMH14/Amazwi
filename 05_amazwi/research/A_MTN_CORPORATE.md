### South Africa (MoMo SA) specifics
- **Launch history (three attempts):**
  1. First launched **2012**, partnered with Bank of Athens SA, Pick n Pay, and Boxer stores; "soon scrapped after poor usage."
  2. **September 2016:** MTN SA announced decommissioning of MoMo in South Africa "due to lack of commercial viability."
  3. **January 2020:** Relaunched (MTN's "third attempt"), this time partnered with **Ubank**. Registered users reached "2.50-million by the end of that year," but only **207,000 active users** out of MTN's 32 million SA customers by December 2020 — described in coverage as slow uptake.
- **Current (2025) traction:** MoMo SA reported **13 million registered users** as of ~April 2025 — "nearly three million new subscribers annually since relaunching in 2020." MTN SA executive quoted as being "'stubbornly optimistic' about MoMo relaunch" (ITWeb headline).

## TOP 10 FACTS THAT MATTER FOR A HACKATHON PITCH

1. **The mini app platform is real and imminent, and Nigeria is first.** MTN Group Fintech + Ant International (announced 9 June 2026) is building the exact "mini app inside MoMo" platform this hackathon is themed around, with fraud prevention and merchant/consumer engagement features, launching in Nigeria "next quarter" (Q3 2026). South Africa is not the named launch market — a pitch that assumes SA gets it first is factually unsupported. (Sec. 4)

2. **MTN's own CEO has publicly said Africa "cannot afford to be left out" of AI, specifically citing the continent's 2,000+ languages and LLMs** — but no evidence exists that MTN owns a language dataset or model. This is a stated priority with no MTN-owned asset behind it: a legitimate, evidence-backed gap for a pitch to fill, not something to claim MTN already has. (Sec. 5)

3. **Ayoba — MTN's last consumer "super app" attempt (35 million peak MAU) — was shut down in March 2026**, explicitly because free-data-driven signups didn't convert to retention against WhatsApp, and because of technical/verification failures. Any mini-app idea that leans on "give people data to get them to use it" repeats the exact failure mode MTN just walked away from. (Sec. 7)

4. **MoMo South Africa is on its third launch attempt (2012 → 2016 shutdown → 2020 relaunch) and still reports "registered" users (13 million), not "active" ones** — the 2020 relaunch's own early data showed only 8% of registrations became active. A pitch for the SA market should design explicitly for activation, not signup. (Sec. 3)

5. **MTN's named strategy is "Ambition 2030" with three pillars: Connectivity, Fintech, Digital Infrastructure**, explicitly "underpinned by... AI-enabled growth" — and MTN Group Fintech's internal framing for this consolidation is "One Big Tech" (Serigne Dioum). Any pitch should speak this exact vocabulary. (Sec. 2)

6. **Fintech is genuinely MTN's fastest-growing, most material engine right now**: FY2025 transaction value crossed US$500 billion (+37.6%), H1 2026 EBITDA margin hit 47.6% (highest since ~2012), and advanced services (lending/insurance, not core payments) are the growth driver — up 31.8% in H1 2026 alone. A pitch tied to "advanced services" (insurance, lending, savings) is riding MTN's actual growth curve, not just payments. (Sec. 3)

7. **MTN's current values framework is "Live Y'ello"** — five values: Lead with Care, Can-do with Integrity, Collaborate with Agility, Serve with Respect, Act with Inclusion — useful for aligning pitch language/tone with MTN's own internal culture branding. Do not use the older 2013 "Vital Behaviours" framework as if current. (Sec. 1)

8. **MTN's purpose statement, repeated consistently, is "Leading digital solutions for Africa's progress"** — this is the safest, most-verified single line to anchor a pitch's opening slide. (Sec. 1)

9. **MTN SA Foundation is actively funding youth digital skills (R185m+, DS4DJ programme, 700 youth in 2026) but has no found programme on language/cultural preservation** — if the team's idea touches South African languages or culture, it is new ground for MTN's CSI, not an existing pillar to reference as already-funded. (Sec. 8)

10. **MTN SA's competitive position is subscriber-second (31% vs Vodacom's 42%) but network-quality-first** (higher speeds and Network Quality Score than Vodacom) under a brand-new CEO (Ferdi Moolman, since 1 Nov 2025) — a narrative of "MTN wins on quality/innovation, not scale" is supportable and could frame why a hackathon-grade mini app matters more to MTN than to the market leader. (Sec. 9)
