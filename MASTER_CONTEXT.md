## 2. THE THREE TRACKS

| # | Track | What it likely means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Everyday Essentials** | Groceries, prepaid electricity/water, airtime & data, bills, school fees, health, food, household. The "boring money" track — highest volume, most defensible. |
| 2 | **Entertainment & Lifestyle** | Gaming, streaming, sports, betting-adjacent, events & ticketing, music, social, food delivery, fitness. The **engagement** track — MoMo's weakest area and its biggest strategic need. |
| 3 | **Travel & Mobility** | Taxis, buses, e-hailing, fuel, tolls, parking, cross-border travel, logistics, delivery. The **hardest and most valuable** track in SA. |

**Strategic read:** in every past MoMo hackathon, *lending and savings* won. The 2026 tracks contain **no lending track at all.** MTN is deliberately steering away from "another credit app" toward lifestyle and commerce — because that is what a super app needs and what MoMo does not have.

## 3. THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT FACT

**June 2026: MTN Group Fintech signed a partnership with Ant International** (the Alipay group) to rebuild MoMo as a **super app with a mini app platform**. First launch: **Nigeria, Q3 2026** — i.e. the same quarter as this hackathon — then rollout across MTN's other markets.

Serigne Dioum (MTN Group Fintech CEO) calls it the **"One Big Tech" strategy**.

### What this means for us
- MTN is not running a charity hackathon. They are **stocking an empty shelf.** A mini app platform with no mini apps is worthless.
- They have bought the *technology*. What they cannot buy is **locally relevant supply** and proof that African devs will build on it.
- Therefore: **the winning entry is the one that looks like a launch partner, not a science project.** Judges are unconsciously asking "could this be app #1 on the MoMo shelf in Johannesburg in six months?"
- Corollary: build something MTN would *feel embarrassed not to have*. Not something clever.

## 4. JUDGING CRITERIA (from the official 2026 T&Cs)

1. Innovation & Creativity
2. Relevance to Fintech Challenges
3. Feasibility & Scalability
4. Technical Execution
5. Presentation & Pitch

No published weightings. **Note:** 3 of 5 criteria are non-code (innovation, relevance, pitch). At a 24-hour event with judges seeing ~12 demos, **the pitch and the story carry more weight than the code**. Budget time accordingly — see `03_build/` when we get there.

### Rules that bite
- **Everything must be built during the hackathon.** Pre-existing projects are prohibited unless the organiser approves. Open-source libraries and APIs are fine.
- ⚠️ **Practical consequence:** we can research, design, write the pitch, prepare the architecture, and get MoMo sandbox credentials beforehand — but **the repo commits should start at the event.** Prepare knowledge, not code.
- 18+, MTN employees and family excluded.
- **IP:** participants retain ownership; MTN receives a royalty-free, sub-licensable licence for marketing/promotional use. *(The T&C text as published also contains the word "exclusive" — worth asking the organisers to clarify on the day. Do not assume.)*
- ⚠️ **Discrepancy to resolve:** the registration form says **24 hrs in person**; the group T&Cs say **48 hours to ideate, build and present**. Ask at check-in.
