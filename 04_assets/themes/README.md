# AMAZWI — theme directions

**Canvas:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3b464f09-c6da-4e97-a831-729d1df53f0d

Four grounds, because flat `#14100E` is the default of every AI-generated dark UI. Each has a source that can be named — which is the difference between a colour and a decision.

| | Theme | Source | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Midnight Shweshwe** | Shweshwe discharge-print indigo — worn across Xhosa, Sotho and Tswana communities | **Recommended default** |
| B | Highveld Dusk | A Joburg winter sunset — violet through smoke-orange | Splash and deck only |
| C | **Red Earth** | Warm clay with raw grain | **Mandatory Day theme** |
| D | Ndebele | Ndebele wall painting, geometry as structure | September / Heritage skin |

## The rule that keeps four themes looking like one product

> **The voice gradient (`#FF5A36` → `#E8267F`) and the money yellow (`#FFCB05`) never change.**
> Only the ground, the surface treatment and the texture move.

## Why A over B

B is the most beautiful and the least legible — small text over a mesh gradient is a contrast fight you lose. A gives you a non-generic ground *and* a stable surface for type. Indigo also makes the ember gradient sing in a way warm black does not.

## Why C is not optional

The app is used outdoors, in queues and at taxi ranks, in hard South African sun where dark UI is unreadable. **A dark-only product is an accessibility failure dressed as a style choice** — and it fails exactly the users the product exists for.
